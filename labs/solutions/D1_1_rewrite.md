# Tagger test set: approval needed for six hours of labelling

Please approve six hours of reception time, three for each of two receptionists, before the end
of next week. They will label 150 new messages, so that the comparison you decide on rests on
enough urgent messages.

## Why

We removed duplicate and old messages from the test set. It went from 520 messages to 400, and
the urgent messages from 112 to 80. Urgent recall, the share of urgent messages the tagger
finds, is the number the head of reception relies on. With 80 urgent messages, a measured recall
of 0.80 has a 95% interval from 0.70 to 0.87. That is too wide to say whether the models beat the
rules by an amount that matters.

## What the time buys

The 150 messages come from the North and East sectors, where most livestock owners write from.
The receptionists use the labelling sheet we prepared. No owner's personal details leave the
clinics' systems.

## What happens next

We finish the new test set and bring you one comparison with its intervals to decide on.
